# Marriage

**Navigation:** *Rules and events → Marriage* — route `/marriage`

## Overview

When enabled (OFF by default), characters meeting the configured level requirement can propose with **Shift+M** or `/marry <name>`, hold a ceremony at **Devias Church** (default Map `2` near `(210, 23)`), customize their couple title, warp to their spouse, and receive a bonus while hunting together in the same party and map.

## Divorce & Public Couples List

- Divorcing deducts the configured Zen fee and enforces the remarriage cooldown (`24h` default).
- Admins can review and dissolve couples directly on `/marriage`.
- Active couples and titles are published at `/website/marriage` and rendered on `home.html`.
