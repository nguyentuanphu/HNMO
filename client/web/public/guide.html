Getting started

  1. Sign in on the account page and click Play now (opens straight at server choice with a one-time ticket, no second password), or log in on the game page, then pick the server and a character (or create one with + Create a character).
  2. You start in town, a safe zone: nobody can attack there and you can't attack either. Weapons go on your back.
  3. Walk out of town and click a monster to fight it. Kill monsters for experience, money (zen) and items.
  4. Pick up what drops (click it), drink potions with Q W when life or mana runs low, and go back to town to store items in the vault.

Classes: what to play

Dark Knight

Close combat with swords, axes and maces; lots of life. The easiest class to start with: walk up and hit. Put points in strength and agility.

Dark Wizard

Spells from a distance with staffs; little life, much mana. Strong against groups once the skills are in. Put points in energy.

Fairy Elf

Bows and crossbows from 6 cells away, and support magic (heal, defence, attack buffs) for the party. Put points in agility.

Magic Gladiator

Fights with a sword and casts spells at the same time; strength and energy both count.

Dark Lord

Sceptres, a horse and a raven; leads parties with command (leadership).

Summoner

Books and sticks, summons and curses; weakens monsters for the party.

Rage Fighter

Gloves and fists, fast close combat. Its strikes walk you up to the monster; Chain Drive, Dragon Roar and Dragon Slasher need sword-form gloves, and the close strikes don't work while riding a horn mount. Dark Side dashes through up to five monsters around the target.

Some classes become available on an account only after another character reaches a certain level, as on the original servers.

Where to level

  • Lorencia — the first town. The spiders and budge dragons right outside are for the first levels.
  • Noria — the elves' town, with easy monsters around it.
  • Devias — the snow town, for characters that have outgrown the first fields.
  • When a monster takes many hits and hurts you a lot, it's too strong: go back to weaker ones. When everything dies in one or two hits, move on.

Items and potions

  • Potions: healing potions go on Q and mana potions on W by default. A slot always drinks the best potion of its kind in your bag and shows how many are left.
  • Wearing items: drag an item onto its slot in the inventory, or right-click it. Item names show their level (+7 …) and extras (+Skill, +Luck, +Option); hover an item for its full tooltip.
  • Excellent items (green names) and ancient items glow; they can't be dropped by accident.
  • Vault: talk to the vault keeper in town to store items and zen. Your items are safe there.
  • Events: the server announces when Blood Castle, Devil Square and Chaos Castle open. Right-click your ticket in the bag to see when the next one opens (Invisibility Cloak, Devil's Invitation, Armor of Guardsman). The window at the event's NPC lights only the castle or square of your level. In Blood Castle break the gate, kill the monsters, bring the weapon back to the Archangel; in Devil Square kill as much as you can; in Chaos Castle be the last one standing. In Devias, Mirage takes you to the Illusion Temple, Lugard to the Doppelganger and Jerint to the Imperial Guardian.
  • Personal store: open the inventory and press S (or the store button). Put bag items on the store and type a zen price for each (right-click an item to change its price), write a store name and press Open. Others see the name over your head and buy with /purchase yourname or by clicking it; the zen comes to you. Close stops selling.
  • Cash shop: X in town. Pick a tab and a category, press Buy under an item; it goes to the storage list on the right. Select it and press Use: it moves to your inventory (seals and scrolls work at once). The coins are free: ask the host for them on the account page.
  • Chaos machine: the Chaos Goblin in Noria mixes items: pick the kind of mix, put the items in (click or right-click), check the recipe, success rate and zen, press the mix button. A failed mix can lose the items. Take everything out before closing.

Playing with friends

One person starts the game folder (Start HNMO, the file to open); everyone opens the game's address in a browser. Over the internet, friends join the host through Tailscale and use the host's Tailscale address. Your progress is saved in the host's folder.

World bosses and weekly rankings (host)

In the admin panel, open World bosses and rankings in the game menu: each boss (monster and map number, spawn spots or an area, every N hours or fixed Sydney times, days of the week, minutes of warning and how long it stays, the English and Vietnamese announcements, the rewards of rank 1, 2, 3, 4–10, participation and the last hit, the boss-hunt points), and the weekly ranking coins (boss hunt, Blood Castle, Devil Square, Chaos Castle, castle siege winners) with their switches, coin types and amounts. Changes count at once. Spawn now brings a boss at once (with the saved settings); the recent kills show their damage tables. The state is kept in save/world-bosses.json. Discord posts need the Discord bridge set up (Discord setup page).

Guild levels and the weekly guild ladder (host)

In the admin panel, open Guilds: levels and ladder in the game menu: switch it on or off, the daily cap per member, each guild XP source (on/off and its XP), the minutes in the game per grant, the castle siege winner's ladder bonus, the levels (XP needed and the buff: EXP %, zen drop %, defense) with the buff's English and Vietnamese name, and the weekly ladder rewards (ranks, coins per member and the master's extra, coin type, zen, items) with the points a guild needs. Changes count at once. The page also shows this week's ladder, last week's winners and the castle siege results. The state is kept in save/guilds.json.

Top races (host)

In the admin panel, open Top races in the game menu: each race's id (keep it while the race runs), English and Vietnamese title, what it counts, the classes (all, one, a table per class), when it runs (every day, every week from Monday, every month, or between two dates written as yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm in Sydney time), the minimum for a prize and the prizes per rank (coins and coin type, zen, items as group number level count). Turning a race off or removing it ends it without prizes. The page shows the live standings and the past winners. Account safety (host)

Choose what needs it (taking things out of the vault, trading, opening the personal store, dropping or selling valuable items: excellent, ancient or from a level), how long it stays entered (0 = the whole session), the wrong tries before a lockout and its minutes, and whether players may change it on the account page. The state is kept in save/account-safety.json; the web bridge tells the game server each web player's address (Relay:SendClientAddress in its appsettings.json).

Gift codes (host)

In the admin panel, open Gift codes in the game menu. New code: the code (letters, digits, - and _, matched without regard to case), a note, public (shown on the website's home page), the reward (coins and coin type, zen, items by group and number with level, count, durability, skill, luck, option and excellent bits), the uses in total (0 = no limit), once per account or once per character, start and end as yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm in Sydney time (daylight saving handled), level, reset and class limits. New batch makes up to 10,000 random single-use codes with a prefix (no 0/O or 1/I/L) that share one reward and its rules; download them with CSV (each code, whether it was used, by whom and when). Codes can be edited, switched off or deleted (their uses and the log stay). The settings: on/off, codes on the website's account page, public codes on the home page, wrong codes allowed per account and window (default 5 per 60 minutes). The redemption log shows who used which code, when (Sydney time) and where. Coins are given once by the redemption's reference (the coins history says "HNMO gift codes"); zen and items wait for the character to be in the game with room in the bag. The state is kept in save/gift-codes.json.

Top up coins (host)

Players can buy cash shop coins by bank transfer on the top-up page: it shows a VietQR code for your Techcombank or Vietcombank account with the amount and the message (e.g. HNMO test2) filled in. Set it up in the admin panel: Plugins, Bank top-up (VietQR, Casso, SePay): your name as the bank shows it, the account numbers, the VND per coin, then activate it. For coins to arrive by themselves, connect your bank to Casso or SePay and add a webhook there pointing to your game's public https address: /topup/casso (Casso, with a Security Key) or /topup/sepay (SePay, authentication "API Key"), and put the same key in the plug-in. Each bank transaction is counted once, even when both services report it. Transfers without an account name or below the minimum are only written to the server log; add those coins by hand (Accounts, Cash shop).

The host can rename the game: in the admin panel (on the host's computer, http://127.0.0.1:8095), open Plugins, find Game name and community links, edit its configuration (the name, and Zalo, Discord or Facebook links shown on the login screen and here) and save. Players see the change on the next page load (within a minute).
