Getting started

  1. Log in with your account, pick the server and a character (or create one with + Create a character).
  2. You start in town, a safe zone: nobody can attack there and you can't attack either. Weapons go on your back.
  3. Walk out of town and click a monster to fight it. Kill monsters for experience, money (zen) and items.
  4. Pick up what drops (click it), drink potions with Q W when life or mana runs low, and go back to town to store items in the vault.

Classes: what to play

Dark Knight

Close combat with swords, axes and maces; lots of life. The easiest class to start with: walk up and hit. Put points in strength and agility.

Dark Wizard

Spells from a distance with staffs; little life, much mana. Strong against groups once the skills are in. Put points in energy.

Fairy Elf

Bows and crossbows from 6 cells away, and support magic (heal, defence, attack buffs) for the party. Put points in agility.

Magic Gladiator

Fights with a sword and casts spells at the same time; strength and energy both count.

Dark Lord

Sceptres, a horse and a raven; leads parties with command (leadership).

Summoner

Books and sticks, summons and curses; weakens monsters for the party.

Rage Fighter

Gloves and fists, fast close combat.

Some classes become available on an account only after another character reaches a certain level, as on the original servers.

Where to level

  • Lorencia — the first town. The spiders and budge dragons right outside are for the first levels.
  • Noria — the elves' town, with easy monsters around it.
  • Devias — the snow town, for characters that have outgrown the first fields.
  • When a monster takes many hits and hurts you a lot, it's too strong: go back to weaker ones. When everything dies in one or two hits, move on.

Items and potions

  • Potions: healing potions go on Q and mana potions on W by default. A slot always drinks the best potion of its kind in your bag and shows how many are left.
  • Wearing items: drag an item onto its slot in the inventory, or right-click it. Item names show their level (+7 …) and extras (+Skill, +Luck, +Option); hover an item for its full tooltip.
  • Excellent items (green names) and ancient items glow; they can't be dropped by accident.
  • Vault: talk to the vault keeper in town to store items and zen. Your items are safe there.

Playing with friends

One person starts the HNMO folder (Start HNMO); everyone opens the game's address in a browser. Over the internet, friends join the host through Tailscale and use the host's Tailscale address. Your progress is saved in the host's folder.
