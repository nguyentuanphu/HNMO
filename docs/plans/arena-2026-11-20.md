# HNMO Arena: battles across servers and seasons (plan, 2026-11-20, Sydney)

## The owner's decisions (pop-ups, 2026-11-20 morning)

- **Fair play:** same template for everyone. A player enters with a temporary arena character of their class: same
  level, stats and gear set per class. Their own items never enter and nothing leaves (no trade, drop, pick-up,
  shop, vault or market in the arena).
- **Modes, all four:**
  - 1v1 ranked duels, open at any time;
  - 5v5 team battles (server vs server, season vs season), at set Sydney times;
  - battle royale, at set Sydney times;
  - guild war, at set Sydney times.
- **Rewards:**
  - arena titles and ranks on the nameplate (the existing custom ranks);
  - arena points, spent in an Arena points shop on the player's own server (the item is made there, new);
  - a website leaderboard (weekly and all time, per mode).
  - No coins.

## How it is built

1. **The arena is its own server program**, as Season 9 is:
   - its own database (`hnmo_arena`), ports +200 and save files in `save/arena/`;
   - started by the launcher when "Arena" is ticked in Host settings;
   - accounts are shared (the same shared account store as Season 9), so every player of every server and season
     can enter;
   - nothing of a player's character or items is copied there.
2. **Arena characters:**
   - The admin panel's Arena page sets, per class: level, stats, skills, and one gear set (items, levels, options).
   - On entry the arena makes (or refreshes) the account's arena character of that class from the template.
   - The template's items are bound to the arena: they can't be dropped, traded, sold or stored, and they are made
     again on every entry.
   - Class balance multipliers (damage and defence per class), set by the admin, keep the classes even.
3. **Rules inside:**
   - no trade, drop, pick-up, personal shop, vault, market, mail, cash shop or gift box;
   - no experience, zen or item drops;
   - potions come from the template and are refilled before each match.
4. **Modes:**
   - **1v1:** a queue matches players by rating (Elo-style), with a best of 3 rounds in a small arena map.
   - **5v5:** scheduled; teams are made by server, or by season, or mixed (the admin picks per schedule entry);
     timed, the most kills, or the last team standing.
   - **Battle royale:** scheduled; a free-for-all on a larger map with a shrinking safe zone; the last one standing
     wins.
   - **Guild war:** scheduled; guilds sign up from their own server (membership read from the shared data); 5 to
     10 per guild.
5. **Results and rewards:**
   - Kept by the arena per account and season week.
   - Arena points are paid into the shared database, so the player's own server can read them.
   - Titles go through the custom ranks plug-in.
   - The website shows leaderboards from the arena's data.

## How players get there

- **In the game:** the "Arena" button (and an Arena Herald NPC in every town) opens the arena window. It shows the
  modes, the next scheduled battles in Sydney time, the player's rating, points and title. The player picks a
  mode and a class:
  1. the client gets a one-time arena ticket from its server (as the website's Play ticket);
  2. it logs out of its server and connects to the arena with the ticket;
  3. the arena character of that class is made from the template, and the player queues or joins.

  **"Leave arena"** brings them back to their own server and character with another ticket.
- **From the start screen:** a third column "Arena", next to Season 6.3 and Season 9, for players who go straight
  to the arena.
- **Alerts:** scheduled battles are announced in the game, on Discord and by web push (the existing event alerts),
  10 minutes before.

## Friends across seasons (owner, 2026-11-20 afternoon)

Within a season, friends already work across that season's servers (one friend server per season program: requests,
online state with the server, letters and friend chat rooms). Friends across seasons (Season 6 and Season 9) come with
the Arena, which already links the season programs: add a friend in the other season, see them online, and write to
them. No trading or item moves between seasons.

## Step 4 done: ranked 1v1 (2026-11-20, Sydney)

- The Arena window (in the Arena): rating, wins and losses (all time and this Sydney week), "Find a 1v1 opponent" /
  "Leave the queue" (F5 52 actions 2 and 3), the top 10 of this week and of all time; it refreshes every 5 s while open.
- `ArenaDuels`: a queue per Arena game server, matched every 2 s by rating (gap 100, wider by 50 for every 10 s waited);
  the pair plays the game's own duel (`DuelRoom`, the duel arena map) with `MaximumScore` 2 (best of 3); the duel's
  `Finished` callback records the result. The duel's zen entrance fee is given just before (arena characters carry
  none). After a duel, the Arena brings its players back to the arena map.
- `ArenaRanking`: Elo (K 32, start 1000), weeks from Monday in Sydney time, leaderboards (all time by rating, this week
  by wins). Kept in the Arena's own state file (`save/arena/`).
- Website: "Arena rankings" section (this week, all time; EN/VI), shown while the Arena runs; data from the Arena's
  admin panel (`api/website/arena`) through `/website/arena`.
- Checked live: two players queued, were matched and played the duel in the duel arena; the first round counted
  (1-0). The scripted second kill didn't land in the headless test, so the end of a duel (the recorded result, both
  ratings, the leaderboard) is checked by `ArenaRankingTest.ADuelResultIsKeptAsync` instead.
