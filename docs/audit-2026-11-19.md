## How it was checked

Two independent read-only review passes (game logic: money, items, packet handlers, saves; web: admin
API, website API, bridge, web client, scripts, workflows), plus my own reading of the ban manager, the
worlds registry, the Season 9 overlay, the language files and a search for third-party names and
secrets. Every finding below was confirmed in the code; the two marked *race* were not reproduced live.

Severity: **critical** = players can create items or coins at will; **high** = exploitable by a player
or loses goods; **medium** = needs an admin, a special setup or a rare timing; **low** = hardening.

## 1. Money and items (game server)

### 1.1 CRITICAL, fixed: escrow "claim" paid the jewels again on every failed claim
- `server/src/GameLogic/PlugIns/Escrow/EscrowContractsPlugIn.cs` (`ClaimOwedAsync`), `EscrowStore.cs`.
- The claim credited a delivery's jewels to the Jewel Bank first, then tried to give the goods. With a
  full bag the goods failed and the delivery stayed stored, jewels included: `Read` hands out a fresh
  copy each time, so clearing the jewels changed nothing.
- Reproduce: put N jewels in the Jewel Bank, make an escrow contract with one bag item and those jewels,
  fill the bag, cancel the contract, send the claim action again and again. Each time: +N jewels.
- Fix: a delivery is taken out of the store first (`EscrowEngine.TakeOwedDelivery`), handed over, and put
  back whole only when the goods don't fit (`RestoreOwedDelivery`). Tests: `StoreSafetyTests`.

### 1.2 CRITICAL, fixed: HNMO features ran while a trade or crafting window held an inventory backup
- Every `GameServer/MessageHandler/Hnmo*HandlerPlugIn.cs` and the chat commands; the rollback itself is
  the classic one (`BaseTradeAction`, `PlayerStorages`, `TalkNpcAction`).
- Opening a trade or the Chaos Goblin keeps a backup of the bag and the Zen, restored on cancel or
  disconnect. The new features took items or Zen in that time and kept their side of the deal.
- Reproduce (one of several): open a trade with a second character, salvage a mount in the Mount Chain
  window (+shards), cancel the trade: the mount is back. Same with the leftovers exchange (coins),
  `/luckycoin`, `/blossom`, Jewel Bank deposits, market listings, escrow contracts, and any Zen price paid
  while the Chaos Goblin is open.
- Fix: while a backup exists, none of HNMO's own messages (F5, sub code 2 and up) are handled
  (`ChatCommandGroupHandlerPlugIn`), and players' chat commands are refused with an EN/VI notice
  (`ChatMessageCommandProcessor`). Mount salvage / craft and the leftovers exchange now also delete the
  consumed items in the database, not only take them out of the bag.

### 1.3 HIGH, fixed: changes to the shared state file could overwrite each other
- `EscrowStore`, `JewelBankStore`, `MarketStore`, `LuckyNumberStore`, `MarriageStore`, and 14 more wrappers.
- Each game server, website request and admin page opens its own wrapper; each had its own lock, while
  "read the section, change it, write it back" has to be one step for all of them. Two players on two
  game servers were enough to lose a deposit, a listing or a contract; two servers could both draw the
  same Lucky Number round.
- Fix: all wrappers of a file now hold that file's own lock for the whole step
  (`HnmoStateStore.SyncRoot`). Test: eight wrappers, 400 deposits, none lost.
- Still open (medium): `ActivityLogStore`, `EconomyStore` and the multi-vault store use their own
  backends and keep their own locks; they need the same treatment.

### 1.4 HIGH, fixed (by refusing): the market and escrow kept only part of an item
- `MarketPlugIn.SnapshotFromItem`, `EscrowContractsPlugIn.BuildItemSnapshot`.
- Only type, level, durability, skill, luck, option and the excellent options were stored. An ancient,
  socketed, harmony, level-380 or wing-option item, or a pet with experience, came back stripped.
- Fix for now: such items are refused with an EN/VI notice (`RefuseItemLosingDetailAsync`). The full
  fix, holding the real item row in a system-owned storage, is listed under "Next".

### 1.5 HIGH, fixed: two website market purchases at once paid once (*race*)
- `server/src/Web/AdminPanel/API/WebsiteMarketController.cs` (`BuyAsync`).
- Balance check and debit were not one step; the second debit was cut off at zero.
- Fix: one purchase per account at a time; the balance is read inside that step.
- Still open (medium): the in-game purchase and a website purchase of the same account can still
  overlap; the VIP, gift box and top-up milestone endpoints use the same check-then-debit shape and need
  the same guard.

### 1.6 HIGH, fixed: an expired timed ban lifted a later permanent ban
- `LoginAction.ValidateAccountStateAsync`, `WebsiteLoginTicketController.BuildBlockResponse` (added by
  Antigravity's audit fix for "expired bans stay in force").
- An account with any old, expired timed ban record was let in even when its state was the permanent
  `Banned` (set in the account editor or by another tool).
- Fix: the expiry only lifts the temporary state. Open (low): characters have a single "banned" state,
  so the same question remains for a character banned by hand after a timed ban ran out.

### 1.7 MEDIUM, fixed: market purchase and escrow create were not failure-safe
- `MarketPlugIn.BuyAsync`, `ListItemAsync`; `WebsiteMarketController`; `EscrowContractsPlugIn` (create).
- Was: a listing stored before its item left the bag stayed when the removal failed (item in both places);
  a failed delivery after payment skipped recording the purchase; the seller's coin result was ignored;
  escrow create never saved the player, so a restart brought the escrowed items back into the bag.
- Fixed 2026-11-19: a listing whose item can't leave the bag is taken back (`MarketEngine.WithdrawListing`,
  unit-tested); a paid purchase is always recorded, a failed delivery goes to the claim list; a failed
  seller payout is logged with its `market:<id>` reference (a later grant of it counts once); escrow create
  saves the player right after the items leave.

### 1.8 MEDIUM, partly fixed: multi-vault switch and the world character transfer
- `GameplayRules/MultiVaultPlugIn.cs` (off by default): the vault snapshot and the database save are two
  steps; a failed save can copy one vault into another. The snapshot is as partial as 1.4.
- `Persistence/Worlds/WorldRegistryService.cs` (`TransferCharacterOnceAsync`, admin action): no check that
  the account is offline; the source delete's result is ignored (the character can end up in both
  worlds); the registry file is written in place; all accounts of both worlds are loaded.
- Fixed 2026-11-19 (world transfer): the account is reserved at the login server for the whole move, so a
  logged-in account is refused and nobody can log in meanwhile (unit-tested); if the original can't be removed
  from the source world, the copy is taken back and the transfer fails; the registry file is written
  atomically. Still open: loading all accounts of both worlds, and the multi-vault switch.

### 1.8a HIGH, fixed: worlds shared one set of rates, and their list was in the program folder
- `Web/AdminPanel.Operations/WorldsOperations.cs` (`ApplyLiveRatesIfHostedAsync`), `AdminOperationsExtensions.cs`,
  `Persistence/Worlds/WorldRegistryService.cs`.
- Saving any world wrote its EXP multiplier into the game's base rate, so every world got it; drop and Zen
  multipliers were stored but never applied. Reproduce: set World 2 to 30x and save: World 1 is 30x too.
- The default world also listed the servers of every other world, so the website showed them twice.
- The worlds list (`hnmo-worlds.json`) was written into the program folder, which a new package replaces:
  after an update the worlds, their settings and the record of used character transfers were gone.
- Fix: a world's rates go to its own servers (the per-server rates the Servers page edits); a server belongs
  only to the world which names it; the list lives with the saved state (an old one is taken along once) and
  is written atomically.
- Note for the owner: worlds still share one database and one game configuration. "Database name" on the
  Worlds page is a label; only the first world has a database behind it.

### 1.9 MEDIUM, fixed (rate limit; the flush is unchanged): no rate limit on HNMO's packets
- Any "request" reads a whole section and any change rewrites and syncs the whole state file under one
  lock; one client in a loop slows every feature.
- Fixed 2026-11-19: each player may send a burst of 40 of HNMO's own packets (F5 group, sub-code 2 and up),
  refilled at 20 a second (`HnmoPacketRateLimiter`, unit-tested); more are dropped. Normal play stays far
  below it. Still open: the state file is rewritten on every change (a debounced flush).

### 1.10 LOW, open
- Spouse warp and the Teleport NPC warp have no state checks (alive, duel, map rules, near the NPC).
- A coin delivery is removed even when the grant failed; amounts above 2,147,483,647 are cut.
- Marriage proposals can be spammed; a re-created character name inherits a marriage.
- Top-up milestone goods are delivered outside the player's own lock.

### Found sound
New rule plug-ins (chaos rates, rate tuning, bonus events, map rules, combat limits, multi-vault,
anti-cheat) are off by default and change nothing at their defaults, so Season 6 plays as before. GM
commands need a game master. Website login tickets are single-use, short-lived and stored hashed. Coin
grants are paid once per reference. SQL is parameterised. The state file is written atomically with a
backup. No update plug-in for old saves was added or changed, so old saves load as before.

## 2. Website, admin panel, bridge, web client

### 2.1 HIGH, fixed: a player's text ran as script in other players' game (stored XSS)
- `client/web/src/ui/partyOptionsWindow.ts` (spot name, leader, map), also admin-entered names in
  `mountChainWindow.ts` and `leftoversWindow.ts`.
- Reproduce: advertise a party with the spot name `<img src=x onerror=...>` (48 characters allowed);
  everyone who opens the Party window runs it, with access to their game session and saved logins.
- Fix: every such text goes through `ui/escapeHtml.ts`. Open (low): limit the spot name's characters on
  the server too.

### 2.2 HIGH, fixed: the host's control page trusted "the connection is local"
- `client/web/bridge/ControlEndpoints.cs`, `Portal.cs` (`/portal/setup`).
- Stop / restart, backup restore, setup and log download were open to anything arriving from
  127.0.0.1: every visitor behind a tunnel or proxy on the same computer, any web page the host has
  open (`fetch('http://localhost:8080/api/control/stop', {method:'POST'})`), and a page whose own domain
  points at 127.0.0.1.
- Fix: `HostOnly.IsHostRequest`: local connection, a local host name, no forwarding headers, no request
  from another site, and a local origin for anything that changes something.
- Open (medium): a per-install secret would be stronger still; the rate limiter still counts all
  proxied visitors as one "local" visitor.

### 2.3 MEDIUM, fixed: anyone could make the server call any address (push subscriptions)
- `WebsiteEventAlertsController.SubscribeAsync`, `EventAlertStore.UpsertSubscription`.
- Any `https://` address was stored without limit and called on every event.
- Fix: only the browsers' push services are accepted, with length limits and a cap of 20,000.

### 2.4 MEDIUM, fixed: the first-run setup wizard
- `ControlEndpoints.cs` (`/api/control/setup`), `control.html`.
- Fixed: it rewrote `hnmo-settings.json` with its eight fields, dropping the database password, relay
  hosts and paths the host had set. It now changes only its own fields.
- Open: the "admin password" it asks for is checked for length and then not used; the admin user is
  still made on the admin panel's `/users` page (the page says so since Wave 1). Either create the user
  from the wizard or remove the field. It can also be run again at any time.
- Fixed 2026-11-19 (night): the field is gone. The wizard says where to make the first admin user (the admin
  panel's `/users` page), and the server ignores a password an older page still sends. Running it again only
  changes the fields it shows.

### 2.5 MEDIUM, mostly fixed: an administrator of the admin panel could write and run files on the host
- `HostSettingsService` (licence upload path, program and data paths are free text). An administrator
  reaching the panel through the website path could write a file anywhere and have a program started.
- Fixed 2026-11-19: the host settings page refuses folder paths that are absolute or climb out of the HNMO
  folder, and a server program with a folder in it (`HostSettingsValidator.IsInsideInstallFolder`,
  unit-tested); the licence upload writes only inside the HNMO folder. Existing settings still load.
- Still open: making the page itself host-only.

### 2.6 MEDIUM, fixed: the e-mail given at registration was trusted by Google sign-in
- `Portal.cs` (`/portal/register`, `/portal/google-ticket`), `WebsiteAccountEmailController`.
- Was: the address typed at registration was stored unverified and not unique; a second account with someone's
  address blocked their Google sign-in, and an account with a wrong address could be taken by that address's
  owner.
- Fixed 2026-11-19: the website's form no longer sets an address (it says to add one on the account page,
  EN + VI); only the confirmation code flow or Google sign-in (an address Google verified) sets it; the
  confirmation flow refuses an address another account has (`email-taken`, before sending and again when
  applying; unit-tested). Existing accounts keep their stored address.

### 2.7 LOW, fixed: the control page put file names and release notes into the page as HTML
- `client/web/public/control.html`. Escaped; the download link must be a GitHub link.

### 2.8 LOW, open
- Password guessing on `/portal/*` is limited per address only, not per account.
- Escrow state shows both parties' login names to each other (half of a login).
- `/portal/coins/{account}` answers without a login.
- `/relay` lets a visitor open a connection to any local port (game packet framing only).
- The admin panel's default website path is `/adminpanel`.

### Found sound
Every new website endpoint for accounts needs the API key and checks the player's login again, plus bot,
ban and ownership checks. The world editor and backups need an administrator. Webhook keys are compared
in constant time; the escrow webhook checks signature, time and nonce. The `/data` file server refuses
paths outside its folder; restore checks archive paths; no process is started from a built command
line. The public website builds its pages with `textContent`. No secrets in the diff; the workflows use
none.

## 4b. Found by the browser tests of PR #85

- **HIGH, fixed: one gift box plug-in never ran.** `GiftBoxActivityPlugIn` had two public constructors the
  plug-in manager could use, so creating it failed and the server only logged an error at every start. The
  test constructor is internal now, and `GeneratedPlugInRegistryTest.EveryPlugInHasOneConstructorTheManagerCanChoose`
  checks every plug-in of the server (it failed on this one and passes now; no other plug-in has the problem).
- **MEDIUM, fixed: the first chat line after loading the page was dropped.** The chat's one-second cooldown
  counted from the page load, so a line sent in the first second (the touch HUD's macro buttons) vanished.
  `mobile-hud.spec` caught it.
- **MEDIUM, fixed (by taking it out): the command window's 13th button.** The escrow work added a "Contract"
  button to the command window (D), which is the desktop's 12-button window: the extra button covered the
  window's close button and ran past its frame (`command-window.spec` caught it), and had no Vietnamese text. A
  contract is started from the escrow window, with the other player's name typed in.

- **HIGH, fixed: F9 opened two windows.** The escrow window (helpers' work) took F9, which the owner gave to
  the top races window (2026-11-16): F9 opened both and the escrow window's backdrop covered the game, and
  nothing else opened the escrow window. It is Shift+F6 now (beside the item market's F6), 📜 in the phone menu
  and `/contract`; its texts, the guide and the Vietnamese menu labels follow.
- **Tests, fixed: the browser tests couldn't answer for the server on pages with the service worker.** The
  installable web app's service worker (helpers' work) answers requests itself, and Playwright's `page.route`
  never sees those (`opus.spec` failed every time). The test configuration blocks service workers; the
  service worker test fetches the script, so it still runs.
- **Timing only (no change):** `party`, `touch`, the skill-effect light case, `account-safety`, `buffs`,
  `events`, `timetable`, `trade`, `movement`, `onboarding` and `pets` failed or retried once on CI and pass
  locally against a fresh demo server. `main` has no green browser run to compare with: its last runs were all
  cut off at the time limit.
