# What HNMO still misses from the Season 6 and Season 9 servers and clients (2026-11-20, Sydney)

Asked by the owner: "I asked many times to take everything from the Season 6 and Season 9 servers and clients, and
something is always missing; check everything." Read-only audit on the owner's PC, at `6b77aead`. Sources:
- `<S6 server files>` and `<S9 server files>`: the two server suites' configuration (the Season 9 one is the folder
  the importer read in Job 8);
- `<S6 client data>` (14,103 files, 881 MB) and `<S9 client data>` (18,965 files, 1.1 GB);
- the repository's base data `client/src/bin/Data` (14,891 files), the Season 9 pack `<scratch>/s9-pack` (9,997 files);
- the desktop client's source `client/src/source`, the web client `client/web/src`, the server `server/src`.

Names, counts and sizes only; no game file is in the repository. Three parts were read by helper agents and the
main findings checked again by hand (marked **checked**). Where a row says "not verified", it comes from file and
keyword searches only.

**In one line:** the Season 6 side is nearly all there, but the importer brings over little and some of it wrongly;
the web client lacks a set of Season 6 windows; and Season 9 has its files in the pack but almost nothing that
points the game at them, and none of its own systems.

## 1. The importer: what it brings over, and its bugs

The admin panel's Import server (`GameLogic/ServerImporter`) reads 7 kinds of data: EXP rates, drop lists, NPC shops,
event times, chaos +10..+15 rates, monster spawns, and (with a database) accounts, characters, vaults and guilds.
Every other setting of the old server stays at HNMO's defaults after an import.

Bugs in what it does import:

| # | bug | where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **checked:** the +10..+15 chaos rates go to recipes numbered 10-15, which are First Wings, Dark Horse, Dark Raven and Potion of Bless; the +10..+15 upgrades are numbers 3, 4, 22, 23, 49, 50 and keep their rates. Only the normal rates are read (not enhanced, socket or luck) | `LegacyServerImportEngine.cs` (chaos rates step); `VersionSeasonSix/ChaosMixes.cs` |
| 2 | **checked:** item drop % and the zen multiplier are read and shown in the preview, but the apply step sets only the EXP rates; the excellent drop % isn't read | apply step, rates |
| 3 | event times go to the timetable display only; Blood Castle, Devil Square and Chaos Castle keep HNMO's start times | event schedule step; `PlugIns/PeriodicTasks/*StartPlugIn` |
| 4 | happy hours (bonus events) also go to the timetable display, not to the bonus event plug-in; end hour, weekday, master EXP and excellent drop are ignored, the length is fixed at 60 minutes | bonus events |
| 5 | box and boss item bags become drops of every monster (`MonsterNumber = -1`) | `ParseItemBags` |
| 6 | shop items, drops and database items keep only level, durability and skill: luck, option, excellent, ancient, socket, harmony and 380 options are dropped | parsers |
| 7 | inventories and vaults from the database are read in 16-byte slots; both servers' database scripts use 32-byte slots, so imported bags come out wrong; resets, master level, skills, quests, PK and Gens state, coins, friends and letters aren't read | `ParseLegacyBinaryStorage` (default 16); database reader |
| 8 | imported spawns are added on top of HNMO's own (nothing cleared), NPC spots included, so monsters and NPCs can be doubled | spawn step |

Settings no import touches (HNMO has the feature; it must be set again by hand): monster stats, item definitions and
options, ancient sets, harmony, sockets, 380 options, skills, class quests, Gens, every event's rules (levels,
rewards, monsters), Kanturu, Raklion, Crywolf, Castle Siege, invasions, PK rules, duel, guild, party, marriage, cash
shop catalogue, VIP, resets, offline trade and levelling, MU helper, pets, anti-hack, news, banned words, map rules,
gates and move list, item prices.

## 2. Server systems HNMO doesn't have

Season 6 (both servers have them unless noted):
- the episodic quest system (51 quest scripts and its NPC lists);
- Chaos Card rewards (the NPC window and storage exist, the card mixes and rewards don't);
- the gambling lottery, Mu Rummy, the egg event and event inventory, the Loren Deep event, appointed drops;
- monster group regeneration (a timed boss with its minions; HNMO's world bosses are a different design);
- staged mining (HNMO has a simple `/mine`);
- an IP allow list and connect-member lists;
- GM commands for Kundun's health (`/kundunhpinfo`, `/setlifekundun`), castle siege commands (`/cs…`), `/setparty`,
  `/endwar`; no per-GM rights table;
- monster AI and monster skill tables (HNMO has its own AI for Crywolf, Kanturu, Doppelganger, Imperial Guardian,
  Raklion);
- PK penalties by PK level (item and zen loss, shop and warp bans, warp cost), per-map VIP gates and entry blocks;
- zen drop per map and per monster level, jewel drop rates by monster level.

Season 9 only (none of these exist in HNMO, nor in the desktop client's source or the server's packets):
- Pentagram and elemental items, Errtels; Muun companions; the 330-level master skill tree; stat specialization;
  accessory (ring and pendant) levels; reset item restrictions; cancel item sale;
- Arca War, Acheron Guardian, Tormented Square, Chaos Castle Survival, Illusion Temple Renewal;
- the Battle Core server;
- the Season 9 maps' server side (Acheron, Debenter, Urk and the others) and about 281 Season 9 items.

## 3. Client data

The base data is close to a full copy of the Season 6 client's. What differs:

| what | gap |
|---|---|
| **Arena (map 6)** | **checked:** the base `World7`/`Object7` is an older Arena: its object file is 44,674 bytes against the Season 6 client's 155 KB, the terrain, attributes and lights differ, and 35 object models (Object42-76) and its minimap image are missing |
| the server pack's custom client folder | 109 files: a custom health-orb HUD and 85 custom jewel models; not in the base data, nothing uses them |
| Kalima's minimap image | missing (`World25/mini_map`) |
| 66 Season 6 item models | in the base data but in no item model table (magic box, skill scrolls, Blood Castle items, snowman, panda, some swords…); not verified one by one |

Season 9 (the pack has the files; nothing points the game at most of them):

| what | gap |
|---|---|
| **monsters and NPCs** | **checked:** Season 9's monster models (Monster218 and up, 33 in the pack) and 43 NPC models are in no monster model table, so Season 9 monsters fall back to the ghost model |
| **maps** 82, 91, 93-95, 98, 100, 102 | terrain and objects load from the pack, but they have no name (banner "Map N"), no map icon, no minimap (Season 9 keeps minimaps in its interface folder and a minimap table, neither served), no gates (**checked:** the game reads the base `gate.bmd`; Season 9's differs), no music or ambience (3 tracks and 102 sounds unmapped), no monster list |
| **items** | 679 Season 9-only item models with no item model entry; Season 9's bigger set, excellent, harmony, mix and socket tables are not read |
| skills and effects | the 37 Season 9 skill and 76 effect files are in the pack but no effect table names them |
| texts and tables (`Local`) | never come from the pack by design (other format); Season 9's own tables (gates, Muun, Pentagram, Mu Rummy, Arca, stat options, help) are unused |
| interface | Season 9's 1,150 interface files (its newer UI) are never served |

## 5. Suggested order

Things a player sees first:
1. The importer's bugs (section 1, 1-8): an owner who imports their old server gets wrong rates, wrong bags and
   broken characters today.
2. Other players' gear changes (0x25) and PK colour; logout and switch character; delete character.
3. Season 9 monsters and NPCs (ghosts), map names, gates, minimaps and music for the Season 9 maps.
4. Guild war; Kanturu and Raklion windows; the Season 6 event NPCs (Golden Archer, Lucky Coin, seasonal gifts).
5. Arena's map data; the missing item windows and social windows.
6. Season 9's own systems (Pentagram, Muun, Arca, Acheron, Tormented Square, 330 master tree): each needs server,
   data and client work; a plan per system first.
