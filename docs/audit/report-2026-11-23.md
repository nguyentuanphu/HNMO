# HNMO Full Audit Report — 2026-11-23

**Branch:** `audit-full-2026-11-23` (from `claude/hnmo-main-handover-54co2m`)

---

## Executive Summary

This audit compared HNMO (the web game server + client) against the Season 6 and Season 9 server suites and full clients across five dimensions:

1. **File inventory** — 34,082 files catalogued across four folders
2. **Data dumps** — HNMO's own initialized data for both seasons (15 tables each)
3. **Data table diffs** — Suite vs HNMO for 10 core data tables
4. **Code unit audit** — All 390 tracked code units in `server/src/GameLogic` reviewed against suite rules
5. **Report** — This document

**Bottom line:** HNMO is a **substantial but incomplete** implementation. It covers the core gameplay loop (combat, skills, items, inventory, party, guild, trade, basic events) but is missing **20 Season 9 maps**, **~132 Season 9 gates**, **148 Season 9 items**, **39 Season 9 monsters**, **18 NPC shops**, and has systematic data differences (Durability=0, DropLevel=0) across hundreds of shared items/monsters.

**Season 9 layer verified:**
- +295 items (1,112 − 817) — catalog defines 290 entries; plug-in comment says "279 unique (281 rows with duplicate 13-229/13-230)"; actual dump = 295
- +111 monsters (615 − 504) — catalog defines 111 entries
- +10 maps (83 − 73)

**Discrepancy explained (281 vs 279 vs 295):**
- `SeasonNineOnlyItemsCatalog.cs` has **290** explicit entries across Weapons (14), Shields (5), Armors (43), Wings (7), Misc (221)
- `SeasonNineOnlyItemsUpdatePlugIn.cs` comment: "279 unique Season 9-only item definitions (281 rows in the Season 9 server suite item table, resolving the duplicate 13-229 / 13-230 entries to Wooparoo / Wooparoo (Evolved))"
- Actual HNMO S9 dump: **295** S9-only items (1,112 − 817)
- **12 items in S9-only dump but not in catalog** (groups 6 and 12):
  - Shields: 6-22 (Lazy Wind Shield), 6-23 (Light Lord Shield), 6-24 (Dark Devil Shield), 6-25 (Magic Knight Shield), 6-26 (Ambition Shield)
  - Wings: 12-262 (Cloak of Death), 12-263 (Wings of Chaos), 12-264 (Wings of Magic), 12-265 (Wings of Life), 12-266 (Wings of Conqueror), 12-267 (Wings of Angel and Devil), 12-268 (Wings of Conqueror)
- **12 items in catalog but not in S9-only dump** (groups 22–26, 262–268):
  - Shields: 22-1, 23-1, 24-1, 25-1, 26-1
  - Wings: 262-2, 263-4, 264-4, 265-4, 266-4, 267-4, 268-4
- **Root cause:** Catalog uses groups 22–26 for shields and 262–268 for wings as **item numbers within their arrays** (not persistence groups). The `SeasonNineOnlyItemsUpdatePlugIn.CreateShield` writes to `ItemGroups.Shields = 6` and `CreateWing` writes to `ItemGroups.Orbs = 12`. The dump shows the actual persistence groups (6 and 12).

## 6. Questions for Owner

1. **Monster stats zero** — Is this expected? The dump shows HP=0, Dmg=0-0, Def=0 for all monsters. Are stats loaded at runtime from `MonsterStatAttribute` / database, or is the dump incomplete?

2. **Item Durability/DropLevel = 0 in the Season 6 / Season 9 suite** — Hundreds of items have Durability=0 and/or DropLevel=0 in the Season 6 / Season 9 suite but non-zero in HNMO. Is this intentional (e.g., "unbreakable" / "non-drop" by design in the suite) or a data initialization difference?

3. **MaxMasterLevel 200 vs 330** — Suite S9 supports 330 master levels. HNMO caps at 200. Is this a deliberate Season 6 parity choice, or should HNMO support S9 master levels?

6. **Skill count gap (87 missing)** — HNMO has 316 skills vs 403 in suites. Which 87 are missing? Are they unused/legacy, or needed for S9 classes?

7. **Testing strategy** — 390 code units, 0 integration tests for major flows (mini-games, castle siege, gens, item market). What's the testing priority?
