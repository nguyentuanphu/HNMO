# Map check: monsters, NPCs and objects in Season 6.3 and Season 9 (2026-11-20)

The owner asked: "check monsters, objects, everything: is the map right and does it show right? Before, a skeleton
showed up as a player character." Every map of the web client was visited in both seasons, and the client's own state
was read rather than pixels.

## How it was checked

- **In the game.** The test GM was warped (`/move <hero> <map> <x> <y>`) to up to nine monster areas per map (the
  areas come from the server's map initializers). At each spot every monster and NPC in view was recorded from
  `window.__game.online.characters`: its number, the model slot it got (`look.model`), why it got the unknown
  monsters' look (`look.unknown`), player-shaped or not and with which skin, its body file, meshes drawn without a
  texture and see-through meshes. Per map also the HTTP 4xx answers, the console warnings ("Model missing", "Texture
  missing") and the object types the client skips. One screenshot per map in
  `docs/screenshots/map-check-2026-11-20/<s6|s9>/` (960 px JPEGs). Season 6.3 ran on the demo world. Season 9 ran on
  the same world set to Season 9 on the admin Worlds page (set back to Season 6.3 afterwards), with the web client on
  port 8084 and the Season 9 pack.
- **Offline, every monster number.** The monster viewer (`dev/monsterViewer.js`) built all 554 looks of
  Monsters.json in each season. The result was the same in both: 0 errors, the ghost only for the 5 entries the data
  gives no model, and the 3 known untextured models.
- **Offline, maps the GM cannot enter.** These are the event maps (Devil Square, Kanturu tower, Silent Map, Raklion
  hatchery, Duel Arena, Doppelganger, Varka) and the Season 9-only maps, which the server does not have yet. Each
  world was built with the game's own `loadWorldData` + `createWorld` and its object meshes were read.
- **Season 9 monster list.** The Season 9 server suite's monster list and spawn list were compared with Monsters.json.

Counts:
- **Season 6.3:** 67 maps tried, 53 entered, 8 more built offline. That covers 61 map folders: Doppelganger 66-68 and
  Varka 70-72 share 65's and 69's folders.
- **Season 9:** 46 maps tried, 30 entered, 16 built offline. Only one map each of Blood Castle, Chaos Castle, Kalima
  and Illusion Temple was tried, because their instances share a folder and all of them were entered in Season 6.3.
- Monster and NPC numbers seen: 224 in Season 6.3, 182 in Season 9.

Checked and found correct:

- **Skeletons.** Skeleton 14, Skeleton Archer 15, Chief Skeleton 16 and Lorencia's Golden Archer 236 are drawn with
  the skeleton skins (`Skeleton01-03.bmd`) in both seasons, in the Dungeon and in Lorencia, and the in-game skeleton
  test passes. The other player-shaped characters are meant to be player-shaped: guards 247/249, Shadow Phantom
  Soldier 257, Leo 371, Chaos Card Master 375, Marlon 229 and Cursed Wizard 34. Their entries say
  `"model": "player"` and give their own equipment.
- **See-through by design:** Ghost 11, Ice Monster 22, Alquamos 69. **No body by design:** the Crywolf altars
  205-209, which are an effect of the map.
- **Missing object models and tiles** in the tables are missing from the original Season 6.3 client's folders too,
  so the desktop does not draw them either. Examples: Tarkan's Object01/04/05/36/39, Noria's Object44, Lost Tower's
  Object41, Blood Castle's Object38, Kanturu Relics' Object22-85, and Swamp of Calmness's Object59/60/65. The same
  goes for object types above 160 (Raklion's type 247, 65 objects, and Elveland's 165), since the desktop's
  `MAX_WORLD_OBJECTS` is also 160.
- `bons.jpg` (objects in Crywolf, Balgass and Vulcanus) is missing from those object folders in the original client
  too.
- The skill-effect textures that return 404 in Lorencia (`Skill/Swordeff_mono.jpg`, ...) belong to effects, not to
  the map.
- The hidden markers of PR #134 stay hidden, and its test passes.
